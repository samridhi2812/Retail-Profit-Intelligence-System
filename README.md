# 📊 Retail Profit Intelligence System

## Overview

The **Retail Profit Intelligence System** is an end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes retail sales data to uncover insights into revenue, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance. The project demonstrates the complete analytics workflow—from data extraction and cleaning to business analysis and dashboard visualization—using **SQL, Python, and Power BI**.

The objective is to help business stakeholders identify high-performing products, improve profitability, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), and make data-driven decisions.

---

## Business Problem

Retail businesses often generate high sales but struggle to understand which products, categories, or regions actually drive profits. Without proper analysis, decision-makers may invest in low-performing products or miss profitable opportunities.

This project answers key business questions such as:

* Which products generate the highest profit?
* Which categories have high sales but low margins?
* Which regions contribute the most to revenue and profit?
* How do sales and profits change over time?
* What are the key drivers of business performance?

---

## Objectives

* Analyze retail sales and profit performance.
* Identify top-performing and loss-making products.
* Evaluate regional and category-wise profitability.
* Track monthly sales and profit trends.
* Build an interactive dashboard for business users.
* Generate actionable business recommendations.
